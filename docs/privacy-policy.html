Claude Usage Monitor - Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 28, 2026

Overview

Claude Usage Monitor ("the App") is a utility app that helps you monitor your Claude AI account usage limits. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what data the App accesses and how it is handled.

Data Collection

The App does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers. All data remains on your device.

What the App Accesses

  • Claude.ai Session Cookies: The App uses an embedded WebView to log in to your Claude.ai account. Session cookies are stored locally on your device to maintain your login session. These cookies are never shared with third parties.
  • Usage Data: The App reads your Claude.ai usage limits (percentage used per model, plan type, reset times) from the claude.ai website. This data is cached locally on your device for display in the app and notifications.

Data Storage

All data is stored locally on your device using Android DataStore preferences. This includes:

  • Session cookies (for maintaining login)
  • Cached usage data (for displaying in notifications)
  • App preferences (refresh interval, notification settings, language)

No data is sent to any server other than claude.ai for the purpose of retrieving your usage information.

Third-Party Services

The App connects only to claude.ai (operated by Anthropic) to retrieve your account usage data. The App does not integrate any analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Data Deletion

You can delete all stored data at any time by using the "Log out and clear session" option in the App settings, or by uninstalling the App.

Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for use by children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you can open an issue on our GitHub repository.
